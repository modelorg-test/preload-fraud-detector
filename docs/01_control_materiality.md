# Document Control & Materiality

## Classification

This model is classified as **Tier 1 Critical** under the CBUAE Model Risk Management framework. It directly impacts regulatory reporting obligations under FATF Recommendation 20 (suspicious transaction reporting) and local AML/CFT regulations.

## Materiality Assessment

| Criterion | Rating | Justification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regulatory Impact | Critical | Directly feeds Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) generation |
| Financial Exposure | High | Potential regulatory fines for missed alerts |
| Customer Impact | Medium | False positives trigger account holds and investigation |
| Operational Dependency | High | 24/7 real-time scoring in the payment processing pipeline |
